FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to speak Hungarian?
Many programs are taught in English. Hungarian language courses may apply for scholarship holders — we explain requirements per program.
Do you charge for Stipendium Hungaricum?
No. The official scholarship application is free. We provide information and guidance only — you apply through the official portal.
How much does self-funded study cost?
Tuition and living costs vary by university and city. We give you exact numbers in consultation — no hidden fees.
Is visa guaranteed?
No agency can guarantee a visa. We help you prepare complete, correct documents to give you the best chance.
Which universities do you work with?
Partner universities will be listed here as agreements are signed. Book a consultation to discuss current options.
