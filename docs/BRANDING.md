# Branding — Final Decision

**Chosen brand:** **Yacine Path Hungary**  
**Display lockup:** **Yacine Path** (primary) + **Hungary** (secondary)  
**Tagline:** *From Algeria to Budapest. Step by step.*  
**Social handle (target):** `@yacinepathhungary` or `@yacinepath`  
**Date:** July 2026

---

## Why this name

| Requirement | How Yacine Path Hungary delivers |
|-------------|----------------------------------|
| Keep "Path" (user liked) | **Path** = journey metaphor — orientation → arrival |
| More punch than "Path" alone | **Hungary** adds destination, searchability, university credibility |
| Yacine-first | **Yacine** is the first word students see |
| Algeria → Budapest story | Tagline + eyebrow carry the route; name stays short |

**Rejected alternatives:** Yacine Path alone (too minimal), Yacine Study Hungary (less distinctive), Budapest with Yacine (Yacine not first).

## Brand voice

**Tone:** Friendly, honest, expert — like a trusted older student who has done the journey, not a sales agent or government brochure.

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Use Yacine's real story and name | Promise guaranteed admission or visa |
| Explain costs clearly (tuition, living, service fees) | Use fake urgency or hidden fees |
| Separate Stipendium (free) from self-funded (paid service) | Charge for or imply paid scholarship applications |
| Show the 5-step journey visually | Copy competitor websites word-for-word |
| WhatsApp as primary contact | Hide founders (no faces, no names) |
| Link to official Study in Hungary / Stipendium pages | Claim official government status |
| Algerian student perspective (FR/AR content for students) | Generic "study abroad anywhere" messaging |

**Language:** English for universities and website v1; French and Arabic for student-facing content (social, orientation video, key pages in v2).

**Example — good:** *"We guide you step by step — from first question to enrolled student in Budapest. No hidden fees; Stipendium applications are free through the official portal."*

**Example — avoid:** *"Guaranteed visa! Limited spots! Apply now before it's too late!"*
