# Orientation Module 1 — Outline

**Title:** Why Budapest? — Yacine's story and your first step  
**Format:** 15–20 min video + optional PDF summary  
**Language:** French or Arabic primary (Yacine's audience) — English version optional  
**Goal:** Build trust, explain path, drive signup for consultation  
**Strategy ref:** Phase 4.1

---

## Before recording — checklist

- [ ] Quiet location, good light, face camera
- [ ] Simple background (Budapest street/campus b-roll optional)
- [ ] No old agency branding visible
- [ ] WhatsApp / website URL ready for end screen
- [ ] Only use **verified stats** (~100/semester, ~1M followers if mentioned)

---

## Script structure

### 1. Hook (1 min)
- "If you're Algerian and thinking about university abroad — I want to tell you why Budapest changed my life."
- Who Yacine is (brief — they likely already follow him)
- What this video is: honest overview, not a sales pitch

### 2. Why Hungary / Budapest (3 min)
- Quality education, English-taught programs
- Lower cost vs Western Europe _[confirm current tuition ranges before stating numbers]_
- EU degree, life in the heart of Europe
- Algerian community already there — you're not alone
- Personal moment: why Yacine chose Budapest

### 3. What the journey looks like (4 min)
Walk through 5 steps simply:
1. Watch this orientation → decide
2. Consultation → pick program + understand costs
3. Documents + application
4. Acceptance → visa
5. Arrive in Budapest

Emphasize: **we guide you at each step** — our company, not the old agency.

### 4. Two paths — be clear (2 min)
- **Stipendium Hungaricum:** government scholarship, free to apply, official website — we explain, we don't charge for the application
- **Self-funded:** tuition + our support service — full guidance

Don't promise either is easy — be honest about requirements.

### 5. Social proof (2 min)
- ~100 students per semester through the pipeline Yacine built
- Real student moment (one story — name + program if permission granted)
- Community in Budapest

### 6. What we offer — our company (2 min)
- Online orientation (free)
- Consultation → document checklist → application support → visa guidance → arrival help
- More universities coming through co-founder's university partnerships
- Algeria office + Hungary expertise — one team

### 7. Who should apply / who should wait (2 min)
- Good fit: motivated, documents ready, realistic budget or scholarship path
- Wait if: no clear goal, expecting guaranteed visa, not ready to study

### 8. Call to action (1 min)
- "Sign up for free orientation series on [website]"
- "Message us on WhatsApp — link below"
- "Next video: exact documents you need"
